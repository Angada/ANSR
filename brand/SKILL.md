## When building anything for this brand
1. Load color tokens from [tokens.css](tokens.css) or [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. Use brand fonts in [assets/fonts/](assets/fonts/) — display + body = Inter (placeholder until ANSR's licensed face is sourced).

4. Follow [brand-guidelines.md](brand-guidelines.md) for usage rules + voice.
